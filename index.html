QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you invite a bestie into your food log.

Is Bestie an AI coach?
No. Bestie is a calorie tracker with a context-aware voice system. It is not a chat coach, recipe app, or nutrition database flex.
What happens if I go over?
No-Spiral restyles Today with the honest number and one next action. It never offers compensation, shame, or a punishment workout.
Do I have to track weight?
No. Weight is optional, weekly by default, shown as a quiet trend, and never placed in a notification.
Who is founding access for?
Adults 18 or older. That is the only eligibility rule. The product waitlist does not select, rank, or exclude people for fit.
Is the waitlist the same as Bestie Founding Supporter?
No. Join founding access is a product waitlist for early access and feedback. Bestie Founding Supporter is a separate community-support path. It is not open, has no price, and cannot enroll anyone yet.
What is a Bestie Founding Supporter?
Community support only. A Bestie Founding Supporter would receive only the listed community benefits after enrollment opens. Support is not open yet.
Is this an investment or ownership stake?
No. It is not an investment and provides no ownership, equity, profit share, dividends, financial return, appreciation, escrow, insurance, governance rights, or claim on Bestie or its assets.
Is Bestie open source today?
No. Bestie is not open source today. Bestie is being prepared for an open-source release. Only verified local artifact facts are shown until the exact source module, license, commit, and source URL are public.
When can someone enroll as a Bestie Founding Supporter?
Not yet. Prices are not set. Enrollment is disabled. This page does not charge and does not open supporter enrollment.
Can a name be listed publicly?
Public name listing is optional. It is a later, opt-in community benefit and is not required to join the product waitlist.
How transparent is the ledger?
The Funding & Spending Ledger is founder-maintained; not platform-verified. It is structured in monthly CAD and currently has no activity.
